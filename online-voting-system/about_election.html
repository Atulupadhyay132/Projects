About Election

1. WHAT IS A VOTING SYSTEM?
A Voting System is the backbone of a democratic process that allows citizens to choose their representatives and leaders.
In India, every eligible citizen aged 18 and above holds the constitutional right to vote, ensuring that every voice contributes to the nation's future.

2. THE CORE PURPOSE OF VOTING
The primary objectives of voting include:
Forming the Government:- Enabling citizens to select the party or candidate they trust.
Expressing Public Opinion:- Providing a platform for people to voice their preferences.
Strengthening Democracy:- Ensuring power remains in the hands of the people.
Empowering Right Leadership:- Granting authority to capable individuals to drive progress and change.

3. WHAT IS AN ONLINE VOTING SYSTEM?
An Online Voting System is a secure, digital platform designed to make the electoral process faster, more transparent, and highly accessible.
Internet-Based Casting: Voters can cast their ballots from anywhere using the internet, eliminating the need to visit a physical polling booth.
Digital Management: Authentication, vote counting, and result management are handled digitally, which significantly reduces human error and the chances of fraud.

4.WHY CHOOSE ONLINE VOTING?

Accessibility: Allows elderly, disabled, or out-of-station voters to participate easily.
Instant Results: Automated counting ensures that election results can be declared much faster than traditional methods.
Environmentally Friendly: Reduces the massive use of paper ballots and physical logistics.
Enhanced Security: Uses advanced encryption and multi-factor authentication to ensure that every vote is unique and tamper-proof.
